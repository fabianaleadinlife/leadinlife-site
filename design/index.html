Design

For organisations that need something built, not bought.

Some situations do not fit a standard programme: a new director inheriting a divided department, a learning function that needs case material its people recognise, a leadership team that has to reset in one day. I design the intervention around the situation, then deliver it or hand it over.

Interventions and retreats

One- to three-day retreats for leadership teams and departments, designed from interviews and diagnostics rather than a template. Twenty-plus delivered across the UN system and in hospitals, companies and foundations.

  • Leadership transitions and team resets
  • Strategy alignment and ways of working
  • Often the entry point to longer team coaching

Case studies and learning assets

Teaching cases, scenarios and facilitator guides built from your organisation's real dilemmas, disguised where needed. Trained in case teaching at Harvard; written to be used, not shelved.

  • Case series for leadership programmes
  • Facilitator and participant guides
  • Scenario-based assessment material

Programme design

End-to-end design of leadership and career development programmes for staff colleges, L&D teams and HR functions — curriculum, sequencing, evaluation and faculty briefing.

  • Emerging leaders and executive programmes
  • Career development for mission-driven organisations
  • Blended and cohort formats

360 debrief programmes

Design and delivery of 360-degree feedback debriefs at scale, so that a leadership assessment becomes a development conversation rather than a report in a drawer.

  • Any major 360 instrument
  • Individual debriefs and cohort synthesis
  • Hundreds delivered across the UN system
How a project runs

Diagnose, design, deliver, hand over.

Diagnose

Interviews, data and a short diagnostic so the design responds to what is actually going on.

Design

A written design with objectives, flow, materials and how we will know it worked. You approve before anything is built.

Deliver

I facilitate, or I brief and support your internal facilitators.

Hand over

Materials, facilitator notes and a short evaluation, so the work lives on without me.

Describe the situation.

A short call is enough to tell whether this is a design project, a programme, or coaching — and I will say which.
