Coaching

For leaders and the people they lead.

Coaching is a structured partnership with one purpose: that you see your situation more clearly and act on it more capably than you could alone. It is confidential, evidence-based and built around your agenda, not mine.

Executive coaching

For senior leaders in transition, under sustained pressure, or stepping into a larger mandate. Six to twelve months, with a stakeholder-centred approach so the people around you see the change, not only you.

  • Leadership identity and presence in a new role
  • Decisions under uncertainty and board or donor scrutiny
  • Working with a 360 or leadership assessment

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

Conversation

Forty-five minutes to understand what you are facing and decide together whether coaching is the right tool.

Contract

Goals, measures of success, confidentiality and, where a sponsor is involved, what they will and will not hear.

Work

Regular sessions, usually every two to three weeks, with practice between them. Online or in Geneva.

Review

A mid-point and an end-point review against the goals we set — with stakeholders where that was agreed.

Questions people ask
Is it confidential?

Yes. What you say in a session stays between us. When an organisation sponsors the coaching, we agree in writing, before we start, what the sponsor will receive — usually the goals and a progress summary, never session content.

Online or in person?

Most coaching is online. In-person sessions are available in Geneva and, for team work, wherever the team is.

What does it cost?

Engagements are priced as a package rather than by the hour, and depend on scope and sponsor. I will give you a clear figure after the first conversation.

How is this different from mentoring or consulting?

Coaching works from your thinking, not mine. Where you need my experience — in technology, in UN leadership, in building a practice — I will say so and offer it, clearly labelled as advice rather than coaching.
