Does coaching work?

The evidence says yes — when it is done well.

I do not ask you to take coaching on faith. Two independent meta-analyses pooling dozens of controlled studies find consistent, meaningful effects of workplace coaching on performance, skills, well-being and goal attainment. What drives those effects is the quality of the relationship and the rigour of the process — which is where I put my attention.

  • Theeboom, Beersma & van Vianen (2014), Journal of Positive Psychology — meta-analysis of coaching outcomes in organisational settings
  • Jones, Woods & Guillaume (2016), Journal of Occupational and Organizational Psychology — meta-analysis of workplace coaching effectiveness

About

Fabiana Mariano Green

I started as a software engineer at eighteen, led technology teams in Brazil and then internationally, and ten years ago moved into executive and career coaching. Since 2020 I have been a faculty member of the United Nations System Staff College, working with young professionals and senior leaders across the UN system. I am an ICF Professional Certified Coach and a mentor coach, and I have debriefed many hundreds of 360-degree assessments.

Both halves of that story matter now. Leaders are being asked to decide, with AI in the room, what to hand over and what to keep human. I bring an engineer's understanding of the technology and a coach's understanding of people to that question.
