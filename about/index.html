About

Fabiana Mariano Green.

ICF PCC·Faculty, UN System Staff College·MSc Information Technology
Portrait

I wrote my first production code at eighteen, in Brazil. For the next seventeen years I built and ran technology: enterprise systems for banks, telecoms and logistics companies across three continents, and then, at the World Health Organization in Geneva, the infrastructure behind who.int and IT programmes reaching seven thousand staff in a hundred and fifty countries. I learned what it takes to lead teams through large, unglamorous changes, and I learned that most technology failures are people failures wearing a technical disguise.

That is what drew me to coaching. Ten years ago I trained as an executive coach, and since then I have spent more than three thousand hours with leaders from over a hundred countries: UN directors stepping into expanded mandates, C-level executives under board pressure, scientific leaders managing a crisis, professionals redesigning the next chapter of their careers. I have facilitated more than twenty team retreats and designed and delivered fifty-plus programmes on trust, difficult conversations, psychological safety and career resilience.

Since 2020 I have been a faculty member of the United Nations System Staff College, teaching and coaching in its executive management and emerging leaders programmes and debriefing hundreds of 360-degree leadership assessments across the UN system.

The two halves of that story have converged. Leaders are now being asked to decide, with AI in the room, what to hand to the machine and what to keep human. I have built the machines and I have sat with the humans. I bring both to the conversation — without hype, and without nostalgia.
